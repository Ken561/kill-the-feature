# Feature Proposal: AI Meeting Summary

**Classification: Portfolio scenario — fictional proposal built for this exercise.**

## Proposal (As Pitched)

> "Employees spend hours in meetings and often can't recall what was decided or who owns what afterward. Let's build an AI feature that automatically joins meetings, generates a summary, extracts action items, and distributes it to attendees and stakeholders. This is a clear productivity win and a highly visible way to show AI value across the organization."

## Why It Looked Attractive

- Meetings are a near-universal complaint — easy to get executive buy-in on the premise.
- The underlying AI capability (transcription and summarization) is mature and relatively low-risk to build technically.
- Highly visible: everyone in the organization would see and could try this feature, making it a good "AI showcase."

## Why It Deserved Real Scrutiny Before Being Greenlit

A feature being easy to build and easy to get excited about isn't the same as it being the best use of engineering time, governance attention, and organizational trust in AI. The rest of this repository is the scrutiny the proposal didn't get before being pitched, applied before committing resources to it.
